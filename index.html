About Us

A private basic school at Oyarifa Tipper Junction behind K. Ofori Company Ltd, committed to affordable, quality education.

Who We Are

AVIS is a private basic school at Oyarifa Tipper Junction behind K.Ofori Company Ltd, Accra. We provide affordable, quality education from Creche to JHS.

Vision Statement

Fostering a love for lifelong learning, encouraging compassionate service and responsible stewardship of God's creation.

Mission Statement

Our mission is nurturing the whole learner's mind, body, and spirit, by creating a supportive and challenging learning environment. Through partnership with families, we aim to equip learners with the knowledge, skills, and faith to serve and lead in their communities and beyond.

Core Values – AVIS

A – Academic Excellence: We are dedicated to providing a high-quality education that encourages critical thinking, creativity, and a love for lifelong learning. Our goal is to help students reach their fullest academic potential.


V – Virtuous Character: We emphasize the development of Christ-like character traits such as honesty, kindness, respect, and integrity. We aim to cultivate ethical and moral leaders who live out their faith in everyday actions.


I – Inspiring Faith: We seek to inspire a deep and personal relationship with Jesus Christ, integrating biblical principles into all aspects of the educational experience. We encourage learners to grow in their faith and to understand and live according to God's word.


S – Service and Stewardship: We teach the importance of serving others and being responsible stewards of the talents, time, and resources God has given us. We encourage learners to actively participate in service projects and to develop a heart for helping others.

Academics

A full GES-aligned curriculum from Creche through JHS 3.

Creche (6m – 2yrs)

Loving care, early stimulation, music & colors.

Nursery & KG 1-2

Phonics, Numeracy, Handwriting, French, Arts.

Lower Primary (1-3)

Full GES + Computer Lab, Library, Sports.

JHS 1-3

Maths, English, Integrated Science, Social Studies, ICT, BDT, French. Intensive BECE preparation.
